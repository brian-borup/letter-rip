How to play

Spell words. Touch a letter and drag through touching tiles (sideways, up, down or diagonally) to spell a word of three or more letters. Slide back to undo a letter. Let go to play it.

Score. A real word scores the total of its tiles, with Scrabble values. The tiles disappear and the ones above drop down.

Watch the stack. Every few seconds a new row of letters falls onto the pile, and rows come faster as you go. If a column is full when a new row arrives, the game is over.
